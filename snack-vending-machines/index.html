Managed snack vending without the day-to-day workload

A snack vending machine can provide convenient access to confectionery, crisps and other packaged products throughout the working day. In a managed arrangement, the site does not need to organise regular stock purchasing or routine machine support itself.

Match the machine to demand

The right machine depends on available space, expected footfall and the type of products users are likely to buy. Where demand includes both snacks and chilled drinks, a combination vending machine may be more practical than separate equipment.

Modern payment options

Compatible machines can support card and mobile-wallet payments. See our cashless vending guide for more information.
