What fully managed vending includes

A managed arrangement can include site assessment, machine provision, placement planning, installation and setup, product stocking, replenishment, routine maintenance and technical support. Compatible equipment may also support contactless payments and remote monitoring. The exact service and equipment are agreed after the location has been assessed.

How site qualification works

Suitability is assessed rather than guaranteed. The main factors are the regular staff or user population, daily footfall, operating hours, shift patterns, venue type, service access, available machine space and expected demand. Workplaces with approximately 40 or more regular users may be a useful starting indicator, while a smaller site can sometimes be viable when footfall or out-of-hours demand is strong. Read the full managed vending qualification guide.

How we choose the right machine

Machine selection starts with what people are likely to buy and when they will use it. A snack machine suits packaged food demand; a drinks machine provides more chilled-drink capacity; a combination machine covers both in one footprint; and a coffee machine serves hot-drink demand. Floor space, usage volume, opening hours and payment preferences are considered before a recommendation is made.

How installation works

  1. Send the site location, type and population or footfall.
  2. VendingNI reviews suitability and clarifies access, space and product needs.
  3. A suitable machine and placement are recommended.
  4. The agreed equipment is delivered, positioned, set up and stocked.
  5. Replenishment and support continue under the managed arrangement.

How restocking works

Refill frequency follows actual usage rather than a universal timetable. Higher-use or multi-shift sites generally need more frequent visits. Product lines can be reviewed over time, and compatible telemetry can help the vending network plan stock and service visits.

Cashless and contactless payments

Compatible readers may accept debit and credit cards, contactless payments, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Cash acceptance may also be available on some equipment. Not every method is fitted to every machine.

Frequently asked questions

Is a managed machine free?

A qualifying organisation may receive equipment as part of a managed service without buying it outright. This is subject to assessment.

Who fills and maintains the machine?

Replenishment and routine support are handled through the managed vending network rather than by the host organisation.

Can a smaller workplace qualify?

Sometimes. Strong footfall, long opening hours or multiple shifts can make a smaller headcount viable, but every site requires assessment.
