Convenient refreshments in care settings

Nursing homes and care homes often operate around the clock. Vending can complement existing catering by giving staff and visitors access to refreshments outside normal service times.

Choosing a suitable location and product range

Placement, accessibility and product choice should reflect the people using the machine and the home's own policies. Potential locations include staff rooms, reception areas and appropriate communal spaces.

A fully managed service

For qualifying sites, VendingNI can coordinate a managed arrangement in which equipment provision, replenishment, routine servicing and ongoing support are delivered through the vending network, so the care team does not need to run the day-to-day vending operation.

Care-setting usage considerations

A machine may support staff, visitors and residents in suitable shared areas, particularly where refreshments are needed beyond normal service times. Assessment considers population, accessibility, supervision, access and the setting's product requirements.

Machines, placement and payments

A combination or drinks machine may suit an accessible communal or staff area. Controls, payment methods and product selection should be agreed for the users and setting. No clinical or dietary suitability is assumed.

Managed service

Stocking and support can be arranged through the vending network. Send staff and resident numbers, visitor patterns, hours, accessibility needs and proposed position.

Discuss vending for your care home

Tell us your location, approximate number of staff and visitors, and where the machine could be placed. We can assess suitability and coordinate the appropriate network route. See care-setting experience across the wider vending network.
