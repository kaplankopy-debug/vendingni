How Much Does It Cost to Rent an Office Coffee Machine?

The cost of renting an office coffee machine follows the machine, the usage and the arrangement. VendingNI publishes no price list. For office coffee machines, including coffee machine rental and lease, the agreement is made once the site is known. Rental and lease terms depend on the site and the machine, across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland alike.

There is no single coffee machine rental price

The cost follows the machine, the usage and the arrangement. One offer may be the machine alone. Another may also cover delivery, ingredients, cups, cleaning and servicing. Two offers are comparable only when those parts are named.

VendingNI publishes no price list, and rental and lease terms depend on the site and the machine.

What decides the cost of renting or leasing a coffee machine

These points move the cost.

  • Format. Bean-to-cup grinds beans for a staff room or reception. An instant cabinet mixes powder for a larger break. What a cabinet does is in how a coffee vending machine works.
  • Cups and breaks. Say how many people are in on a normal day, and whether drinks bunch into one break. Count people actually in if attendance is hybrid.
  • Water. Ask if the machine is plumbed in or uses a tank, and who fills a tank.
  • Milk. Some machines use fresh milk and others use powdered milk. Fresh milk needs more attention, so ask which the machine uses and who looks after it.
  • Ingredients and cups. Ask who buys them, and whether the machine drops a cup or expects a mug.
  • Cleaning and descaling. Ask who does each.
  • Servicing. Ask what happens after a fault. VendingNI services the machines it manages. It does not repair machines it does not manage.
  • Payment. Free-vend means nothing to pay at the machine, where the site agrees that. Ask who supplies a cashless reader and who pays any transaction fees. Card and contactless payments depend on the machine and the reader.
  • Delivery and installation. Ask if getting the machine into place is included, and mention stairs, a lift and space clear of a walkway.
  • Length and exit. Ask how long the agreement runs and what the exit terms are.

Rental, lease or hire

The three words overlap. Coffee machine rental usually means using a machine without buying it. Hire is often the same idea, and coffee machine lease is also use of a machine without buying it. VendingNI offers rental and lease. Neither is the free option.

Ask who restocks, who cleans and who collects the machine at the end. If nobody on site will do that work, say so. The format and the arrangement are in how to choose an office coffee machine. Snack and drinks machines are covered in the vending machine hire cost guide.

Costs that do not appear on the invoice

Someone still orders ingredients, fills the machine, cleans it and reports faults. That is staff time. If the hot drinks stop, people leave the building or bring drinks in, and out-of-date stock sits with whoever orders. Ask who carries that work. On a fully managed machine, replenishment and routine servicing sit with the service.

When an office coffee machine costs the site nothing

For a qualifying workplace, VendingNI can place a fully managed machine. There is no equipment purchase and no installation charge. Stocking and routine servicing are part of the service, and the machine remains part of that service. The organisation does not normally run day-to-day restocking. Every site is assessed before a machine is placed. About 40 regular users is a typical starting guideline, subject to assessment. A smaller site can still be assessed where footfall, long opening hours or shift demand creates steady use.

Only the managed arrangement is free, and only where the workplace qualifies. How a free placement is assessed is in how to get a free vending machine. The service is fully managed vending. Whether people pay, or take drinks on free-vend, is agreed for the machine.

Questions to ask any supplier before you sign

Ask these before you sign. The answers are what make two offers comparable.

  1. Is this rental, lease, hire, or a fully managed placement?
  2. Is the machine bean-to-cup or a hot-drinks cabinet, and which drinks are agreed for it?
  3. What does the charge include, and what is left with the site?
  4. Who buys the ingredients and the cups, and who restocks them?
  5. Is the water plumbed in or held in a tank, and who fills a tank?
  6. Is the milk fresh or powdered on this machine, and who looks after it?
  7. Who cleans the machine, and who descales it?
  8. What happens after a breakdown, and does support apply only to machines the supplier manages?
  9. Is payment free-vend or a cashless reader, and who pays any card fees?
  10. How long does the agreement run, and what are the exit terms?
  11. Could this workplace qualify for a fully managed machine instead?

Northern Ireland and the rest of the UK

Enquiries are assessed the same way across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Managed coffee for workplaces in Northern Ireland is on coffee vending machines in Northern Ireland. The same cabinet service for workplaces in England, Scotland and Wales is on coffee vending machines across the UK. Rental, lease and the fully managed option are on office coffee machines. Whether a building qualifies is decided for that site. Service coverage in the Republic of Ireland continues as the Ireland pages state.

Coffee machine rental cost: FAQs

Can you rent a coffee machine for an office?

Yes. A business can rent an office coffee machine instead of buying it. VendingNI offers rental, and also lease. What is included, beyond the machine, depends on the site and the machine. Every enquiry is assessed.

Is it cheaper to rent or lease a coffee machine?

It depends on the agreement and on usage. VendingNI offers rental and lease, and both mean using a machine without buying it. What is included, who does the daily work, and how the agreement ends all change the comparison. There is no single figure.

What is included in a coffee machine rental?

That depends on the site and the machine. Use of the machine is the starting point. Delivery, ingredients, cups, cleaning, servicing and a cashless reader may be included or left with the site. On a qualifying fully managed machine, stocking and routine servicing are part of the service, with no equipment purchase and no installation charge.

Can a coffee machine be free for a workplace?

For a qualifying workplace, yes. There is no equipment purchase and no installation charge. Stocking and routine servicing are part of the service, and the machine remains part of that service. Every site is assessed. Only the managed arrangement is free, and only where the workplace qualifies.

How much does it cost to lease a coffee machine?

There is no published figure. A coffee machine lease depends on the site, the machine and what the agreement includes, just as a rental does. VendingNI publishes no price list. Enquiries are assessed the same way across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland.
