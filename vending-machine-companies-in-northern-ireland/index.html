What to compare between vending suppliers

Managed service

Ask who handles refills, product changes, servicing and faults after installation.

Local coverage

Confirm that the supplier genuinely services your location and can support the machine after installation.

Machine suitability

A supplier should match capacity and configuration to space and expected demand rather than offer one standard machine to every site.

Payment options

For many modern workplaces, card and mobile-wallet acceptance is now an important practical requirement.
