Accessible refreshments across long operating hours

Healthcare environments operate around the clock. Vending can supplement existing catering by providing self-service snacks and drinks for staff and visitors when other facilities are unavailable.

Machine placement, capacity, accessibility and product selection should be considered carefully for the specific site and anticipated usage.

Healthcare usage considerations

Hospitals and healthcare sites can have recurring staff and visitor traffic across extended hours. Suitability depends on population, permitted access, site requirements and service logistics.

Machines, payments and placement

Snack, drink or combination machines may serve waiting, staff or circulation areas where placement is approved. Contactless payment can reduce reliance on cash. Product selection should reflect site requirements without implying health or clinical benefits.

Managed service

Replenishment and technical support can be managed around access rules and usage. Send footfall, operating hours, approved placement and product requirements.

Tell us about your location

Email the site location, approximate number of people using the premises and the type of vending service you are looking for. We can then assess suitability for the managed service.
