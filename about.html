Meet the Founder

VendingNI was established with a straightforward mission: to provide businesses, offices, fitness facilities, and public venues across Northern Ireland with high-end, hassle-free vending solutions that require zero capital outlay.

We take complete care of everything from professional installation and modern cashless payment setup to regular scheduled stocking and maintenance.
