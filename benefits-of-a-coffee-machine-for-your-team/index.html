Benefits of a Coffee Machine for Your Workplace

A workplace coffee machine can make hot drinks more convenient for employees and visitors, particularly where there is no permanent café or canteen.

Convenience during the working day

On-site coffee reduces the need for staff to leave the premises simply to buy a hot drink and can make refreshments available beyond normal catering hours.

Useful for different workplace sizes

The right solution depends on staff numbers, expected drink volumes, available space and the type of drinks required. A small office and a large factory should not automatically use the same equipment.

Self-service availability

Automated coffee equipment can provide consistent access without requiring a dedicated member of staff to prepare every drink.

Combine coffee with snack and cold-drink vending

For larger workplaces, hot drinks can form part of a wider vending setup alongside snacks and chilled beverages. Contact VendingNI to discuss the options suitable for your site.
