A convenient amenity for residents

Where a development has sufficient usage and an appropriate communal location, vending can provide residents with access to snacks and drinks throughout the day.

Where can a machine go?

Potential locations include reception areas, communal lounges and other secure shared spaces, subject to access, power, floor space and the building's own management requirements.

Managed operation

For qualifying sites, VendingNI can handle the machine, product replenishment and ongoing support rather than leaving stock management to the property team.

Want to discuss your site?

Tell us the location, approximate number of people using the premises and what you would like the vending machine to provide. We can then assess the most suitable option.
