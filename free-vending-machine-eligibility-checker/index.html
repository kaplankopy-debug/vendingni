What would users want?

How many employees do you need for a vending machine?

Approximately 40 regular workplace users is a useful starting indicator, but it is not a hard rule. Footfall, shifts, long opening hours and concentrated demand can make smaller workplaces suitable.

Can a factory with fewer staff qualify?

Yes. A smaller factory or warehouse may still look promising where multiple shifts or 24-hour operation creates steady demand across the day.

What about hotels and apartment buildings?

Approximately 50 hotel rooms and 200 apartments are indicative guidelines. Occupancy, common-area placement, access and expected use remain important.

What makes a location suitable?

Regular users, visible placement, convenient access, useful product demand and practical replenishment all help. Include your site type, postcode, population, opening hours, shifts, footfall and preferred products when enquiring.
