# Competitor Proof Gap Matrix

Internal benchmark completed 1 September 2026. Scores: **Strong**, **Moderate**, **Limited**, or **Not evidenced**. Competitor claims are recorded only as information-pattern benchmarks, not copied into public content.

Sources reviewed: Selecta Ireland, Causeway Vending, Castle Vending, Coffology Ireland, Vending Ireland/BDS, and current fulfilment-network public pages. LP Vending, Vend NI and Snax Vending did not yield enough stable accessible evidence for a confident detailed score.

## Gaps closed in this pass

- Explained the VendingNI-versus-network operating model.
- Added visible evidence for the managed operational chain.
- Explained what telemetry can and cannot establish.
- Added attributed sector examples without transferring customer ownership.
- Connected proof content to service, qualification, machines, sectors and FAQ pages.

## Remaining gaps not safe to close

The main remaining advantages held by larger competitors are named first-party case studies, independently owned certifications, published service-level commitments, detailed model catalogues and first-party operational scale. VendingNI should close these only when it has its own verifiable evidence.
