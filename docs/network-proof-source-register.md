# Network Proof Source Register

Verified: 1 September 2026. This internal file records provenance and attribution boundaries for public network-experience claims. It is not a sales page and must not be linked from navigation, `llms.txt` or any sitemap.

## Attribution rules

- VendingNI is the customer-facing enquiry and coordination route.
- Operational capabilities below apply to the wider vending partner network unless a separate VendingNI source proves otherwise.
- Conditional technology is never described as universal.
- Partner customers, staff, fleet size, insurance, certifications and accreditations are never transferred to VendingNI.

## Verified service capabilities

### Managed operating chain

- **Fact:** Full-service vending can cover installation, stocking, replenishment, complaint resolution, maintenance and ongoing account support.
- **Public wording used:** Managed fulfilment can include installation coordination, initial stocking, replenishment, maintenance and technical support through the partner network.
- **Sources:** https://vending-machines.ie/faq/ and https://vending-machines.ie/fully-managed-service/
- **Applies to:** partner network only; conditional on the agreed arrangement.

### Product review and replenishment

- **Fact:** Remote sales, product and stock data can support refill planning and product-line review on compatible systems.
- **Public wording used:** Compatible monitoring can help plan replenishment and show which products are selling; frequency still varies by demand and access.
- **Sources:** https://vending-machines.ie/faq/ and https://vending-machines.ie/fully-managed-service/
- **Applies to:** conditional network capability.

## Qualification facts

- **Workplaces:** approximately 40 regular employees or strong customer traffic. Source: https://vending-machines.ie/faq/. Partner-network guideline; assessment required.
- **Hotels:** approximately 50 rooms. Source: https://vending-machines.ie/faq/. Partner-network guideline; assessment required.
- **Apartment buildings:** approximately 200 apartments. Source: https://vending-machines.ie/faq/. Partner-network guideline; assessment required.
- **Exceptions:** strong footfall can make lower-headcount sites viable. Source: https://vending-machines.ie/faq/. Conditional.

## Sector experience

- **Verified sectors:** offices, hotels, gyms/dance studios, nursing homes, manufacturing plants, hospitals/medical centres, schools, airports, residential buildings, construction sites and other high-footfall venues.
- **Sources:** https://vending-machines.ie/product/optional-elevator/ and venue navigation on current partner product/service pages.
- **Public use:** generic sector experience only. No partner customer is described as a VendingNI customer.

## Technology facts

- **Payments:** card, contactless, phone/mobile-wallet and cash capability are publicly described. Sources: https://vending-machines.ie/fully-managed-service/ and https://vending-machines.ie/faq/. Conditional on equipment and reader.
- **Telemetry:** compatible systems can report stock, faults, product selections, transactions, inventory, cash, alerts and reports. Sources: https://vending-machines.ie/fully-managed-service/ and https://vending-machines.ie/cashless-payment-solutions/. Conditional.
- **Touchscreens and remote operation:** supported by selected product pages, but not used as a universal service claim.

## Operational-documentation facts

- **Fact:** A fulfilment partner publishes installation method statements, professional risk assessments, health-and-safety documentation, complaints/returns and service-support policies.
- **Sources:** https://vending-machines.ie/document-library/, https://vending-machines.ie/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/METHOD-STATEMENT.pdf and https://vending-machines.ie/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Professional-Risk-Assessment.pdf.
- **Public wording used:** selected fulfilment partners maintain documented installation and site-safety processes.
- **Applies to:** partner only. No certification or insurance is attributed to VendingNI.

## Case/example facts

- Public media identifies vending examples in offices, nursing homes, a large hotel near Dublin Airport, construction sites, a swimming-pool waiting area and gym/dance settings.
- Source: https://vending-machines.ie/product/optional-elevator/.
- Public VendingNI use is limited to anonymised venue-pattern examples and never states that VendingNI installed or operated them.
- No numerical outcome, revenue figure or testimonial text is used.

## Facts deliberately not used

- Partner fleet size, turnover, employee information, insurance limits, certifications and accreditations: partner-only credentials.
- Signing bonus, trial-period terms and universal no-cost wording: may not apply to VendingNI enquiries.
- Response-time, next-day contact and 24/7 support claims: no VendingNI-wide SLA verified.
- Testimonials and named partner customers: ownership and consent cannot be transferred.
- Product-page claims sourced from third-party manufacturers without clear applicability: unsuitable as general service claims.
- Exact installation duration, machine dimensions and power demand: model- and site-dependent.
