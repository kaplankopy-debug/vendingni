Drinks vending machines

Drink Vending Machines in Northern Ireland

Cold-drink vending for qualifying sites in Northern Ireland, where chilled drinks are the main demand and the machine is looked after as a managed service.

Drink machines, chosen without a published daily rating

No drinks model on this site is published with a bottles-per-day figure. The decision is whether drinks need their own cabinet, and which named equipment family the placement uses.

Cold-drink cabinet

A dedicated drinks machine holds water, soft drinks, energy drinks and other chilled beverages, adjusted to the site. It suits gyms, factories, workplaces and venues where drink demand is stronger than snack demand.

SandenVendo equipment

VendingNI supplies SandenVendo equipment. Size and configuration follow the space, the expected usage and the products. No cups-per-day rating is published for it.

Shared footprint

Where one cabinet must carry snacks as well as cans and bottles, a combination machine is the comparison. The Sanden Vendo G-Snack is the named snack and combination example, not a drinks-only rating.

How you get a drinks machine

For a qualifying site the drinks machine is supplied, filled and supported. The building does not run a retail counter to keep cans cold.

  • The machine is supplied and installed once a placement is agreed
  • The first fill, later replenishment and product changes are handled for the site
  • Routine servicing, maintenance and technical support sit with the managed service
  • Card, contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay depend on the machine and the reader

Refill frequency follows sales, season, capacity and opening hours. Hot drinks are a different machine: see coffee vending. Northern Ireland coverage includes Belfast, Antrim, Lisburn, Dungannon, Mallusk and other locations.

Who a drinks machine suits, by site size

Workplaces

About 40 regular users is the usual workplace starting point. A smaller office can still be assessed when visitor footfall or long hours create steady drink sales. Hybrid attendance changes the number of people actually on site.

Gyms and shift sites

Membership or headcount on paper is a weak guide. Daily visits, evening peaks and the number of people on each factory shift tell you whether a drinks cabinet will be used.

Busy venues

Hotels are often discussed from about 50 rooms, and high-footfall sites from passenger or visitor flow. Those figures are guidelines for assessment, not a guarantee, and they are not a bottles-per-day rating.

Questions people ask

What does it cost?

There is no tariff on this page. For a qualifying location, a fully managed placement means no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. That result is not guaranteed for every enquiry. The hire cost guide and the cost comparison describe the shape of the cost — the machine, the stock and the staff time — without a price list.

Which drinks setup should we ask for?

Ask for a drinks machine when the queue is for cold drinks. Ask for a combination machine when snacks and drinks must share one position. Ask for coffee separately when the complaint is hot drinks. Payment methods — debit cards, contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay — depend on the reader fitted to that machine.

What does servicing include?

On a machine in the managed service, servicing can include installation, replenishment, product changes, routine maintenance and technical support after a fault is reported through the agreed contact. Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. No response time is published. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status. It is not on every machine. Cleaning requirements are agreed for the chosen setup.

Tell us about drink demand

Email the location, how many people use it at the busy point of the day, the hours, and whether you need water and soft drinks, snacks as well, or hot drinks too.
