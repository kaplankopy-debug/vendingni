Cold drinks available when people need them

A dedicated drinks machine can be useful in offices, factories, gyms, hotels and public-facing venues where demand for chilled beverages is high. Combination machines may be more appropriate where one unit needs to provide both snacks and drinks.

Cashless payment options

Modern vending equipment can support debit cards and mobile wallets such as Apple Pay and Google Pay, reducing reliance on coins and notes.

Product selection

The range can be adjusted around the site and its users. Depending on demand this may include water, soft drinks, energy drinks and other chilled beverages.
