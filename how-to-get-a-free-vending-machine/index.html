What “free vending machine” means

Our free managed model is different from simply giving away a machine. The equipment remains part of a managed vending service. For an eligible site, there is no machine purchase or installation charge, and VendingNI looks after stocking and routine support.

Which sites are likely to qualify?

Suitability depends on factors such as expected footfall, regular usage, available space, site access and the type of products required. A larger office, factory, gym, hotel or other busy venue is generally more suitable than a very low-footfall location.

What happens after you enquire?

1. Site details

You tell us where the premises is, roughly how many people use it and what type of vending you need.

2. Suitability check

We assess whether the expected demand and space are appropriate for a fully managed machine.

3. Installation & management

Once agreed, installation, replenishment and ongoing support are coordinated through the appropriate vending-network arrangement.

What is included?

For qualifying sites, the managed service can include the machine, delivery and installation, stocking, refill visits, routine servicing and support. The exact setup is agreed for the individual location.

Can smaller sites qualify?

Yes, in some circumstances. A workplace below the approximate headcount guideline may still be viable when it has strong customer traffic, multiple shifts, long opening hours or concentrated demand. Suitability cannot be confirmed from headcount alone.

Why some locations do not qualify

A managed placement may not be viable where expected usage is too low, the proposed position is hard to see or access, servicing is impractical, there is insufficient safe space, or projected sales cannot support regular replenishment and maintenance.

Information needed for an assessment

  • Organisation name and postcode or location
  • Site type and approximate staff, resident, room, member or student population
  • Daily footfall, opening hours and shift patterns where known
  • Preferred products and machine type
  • Proposed placement, access details and available space
  • Your name and contact email

What happens after you enquire?

  1. VendingNI reviews the information and asks for anything missing.
  2. The site is assessed for likely demand, access and machine suitability.
  3. If viable, a machine type and managed-service arrangement are proposed.
  4. Placement, setup and stocking are agreed before installation.

Where can I put a free vending machine?

A free machine, in this service, is a fully managed placement: no equipment purchase and no installation charge for a qualifying location, with the equipment remaining part of the service. It can be considered for offices, factories and warehouses, schools and colleges, hospitals, gyms, hotels, care homes and shared workspaces. Apartment buildings and transport sites are assessed when footfall is steady enough to support replenishment. The sector pages explain how each setting uses a machine. This page is about whether the location itself can take one.

The assessment looks at staff numbers, visitor footfall, space, delivery access, preferred products, usage, hybrid attendance and break patterns. About 40 regular users is a typical starting guideline for a workplace, subject to assessment. A hotel, a school or a gym is not judged on that workplace figure alone. Occupancy, class times, membership peaks and shift patterns tell you more than a single headcount. A smaller site can still be worth assessing when opening hours are long or visitors create consistent use. A large site can still be unsuitable if the only position is hidden, unsafe or impossible to replenish.

Coverage for this assessment is across the UK (England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland). Northern Ireland is the home region. Republic of Ireland coverage is unchanged from the Ireland pages. Free vending machine FAQs

What does free mean for a vending machine?

For a qualifying location there is no equipment purchase and no installation charge. The machine stays part of a managed vending service. VendingNI looks after stocking and routine support. A placement is subject to assessment and is not guaranteed for every enquiry.

Which kinds of site can be assessed?

Offices, factories, warehouses, schools, colleges, hospitals, gyms, hotels, care homes and shared workspaces. Apartment buildings and busy transport sites can be assessed where footfall, space and access make replenishment practical.

What do you look at before agreeing a placement?

Staff numbers, visitor footfall, space, delivery access, preferred products, usage, hybrid attendance and break patterns. About 40 regular users is a typical starting guideline for a workplace, subject to assessment. It is not a pass mark for every site type.

What is included if the site qualifies?

Machine supply and installation, planned replenishment and product changes, and routine servicing, maintenance and machine support. Cashless and contactless payments, and monitoring, are available on suitable equipment. Service delivery is supported through an established vending network.

Do you only assess sites in Northern Ireland?

No. Qualifying workplaces and venues are assessed across the UK (England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland). Northern Ireland is the home region, and Republic of Ireland coverage continues as the Ireland pages describe.
