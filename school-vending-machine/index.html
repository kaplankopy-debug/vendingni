School vending machines

School Vending Machines, Fully Managed

School vending machines for schools, colleges and other education sites. Products and payment follow that site’s rules. Qualifying sites can take a fully managed service.

Get in Touch Now!

Education machines, with no assumed product policy

No school model is published with a vends-per-day figure. One product policy is not assumed for every school. The machine is snack, drinks or combination equipment in an approved shared area.

Snack, drinks, or both

An approved shared area may suit a snack, drinks or combination machine. The Sanden Vendo G-Snack is the named snack and combination example.

SandenVendo equipment

VendingNI supplies SandenVendo equipment. Size and configuration follow the space, the expected usage and the products. No cups-per-day rating is published for it.

Payments

Card and mobile-wallet payments can reduce cash handling for staff, students and visitors where the site wants that. Cashless options depend on the equipment and the site’s rules.

How an education site gets the service

Product selection, replenishment and support are agreed for the approved setup. The school does not have to run the machine day to day.

  • The machine is supplied and installed once a placement is agreed
  • The first fill, later replenishment and product changes are handled for the site
  • Routine servicing, maintenance and technical support sit with the managed service
  • Card, contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay depend on the machine and the reader

Age group, safeguarding, opening hours and catering arrangements differ widely. The product range is discussed with the organisation and adjusted to the people using the machine.

Which education sites it suits, by size

Who is in the building

There is no published pupil-count threshold. Assessment uses student and staff population, who is actually in, permitted access, hours, supervision and the institution’s product rules.

Not an office headcount

A school is not judged as if it were a workplace of 40 staff. The Lisburn note on the Northern Ireland page makes the same point: use the people in the building and the site’s own rules.

Approved placement

The machine goes in a shared area the institution has approved, where it can be supervised and replenished.

Questions people ask

What does it cost?

There is no tariff on this page. For a qualifying location, a fully managed placement means no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. That result is not guaranteed for every enquiry. The hire cost guide and the cost comparison describe the shape of the cost — the machine, the stock and the staff time — without a price list.

Which school setup should we ask for?

Describe the age group, who may use the machine, the access rules, the hours and the products the site will allow. Snack, drinks or combination equipment is then matched to that brief.

What does servicing include?

On a machine in the managed service, servicing can include installation, replenishment, product changes, routine maintenance and technical support after a fault is reported through the agreed contact. Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. No response time is published. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status. It is not on every machine. Cleaning requirements are agreed for the chosen setup.

Tell us about the education site

Email the location, approximate student and staff numbers, the age group, and what the site will allow the machine to sell.
