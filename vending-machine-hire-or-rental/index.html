Who the managed route suits, by site size

The managed route suits a site with recurring use that does not want to own the stock or arrange repairs. It is a poor fit when usage is too low, the only position is impractical, or the organisation wants to own the asset.

Questions people ask

What does it cost?

For a qualifying location, a fully managed placement means no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. Every site is assessed before a machine is placed. The hire cost guide and the cost comparison describe the shape of the cost — the machine, the stock and the staff time.

Which route should we ask for?

Ask for the fully managed service if you want refreshments without running the machine. Say so if you specifically need a short-term hire or you want to own the equipment. Include the location, how many people use the premises, and whether you need snacks, drinks, a combination cabinet or coffee.

What does servicing include?

On a machine in the managed service, servicing can include installation, replenishment, product changes, routine maintenance and technical support after a fault is reported through the agreed contact. Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status, and that is confirmed with the machine. Cleaning requirements are agreed for the chosen setup.
