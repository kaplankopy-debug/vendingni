Fully managed, hire, or a purchase

Hire and rental are equipment-first arrangements. You typically pay to use a machine for a period. Who fills it, who looks after the reader and who pays for repairs depends on the contract. Buying means the organisation owns the machine and usually runs it. VendingNI’s core route is different: for a qualifying location the machine is supplied and the stocking and routine support are part of the service. There is no published hire tariff.

Who the managed route suits, by site size

The managed route suits a site with recurring use that does not want to own the stock or arrange repairs. It is a poor fit when usage is too low, the only position is impractical, or the organisation wants to own the asset.

Below the usual workplace guideline

About 40 regular workplace users is a starting point, not a rule. A smaller site can still be assessed when footfall, long opening hours or shift demand creates steady use. Hybrid attendance means the number of people actually in the building matters more than the payroll.

Questions people ask

What does it cost?

There is no tariff on this page. For a qualifying location, a fully managed placement means no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. That result is not guaranteed for every enquiry. The hire cost guide and the cost comparison describe the shape of the cost — the machine, the stock and the staff time — without a price list. People who searched for a weekly or monthly hire rate will not find one here.

Which route should we ask for?

Ask for the fully managed service if you want refreshments without running the machine. Say so if you specifically need a short-term hire or you want to own the equipment. Include the location, how many people use the premises, and whether you need snacks, drinks, a combination cabinet or coffee.

What does servicing include?

On a machine in the managed service, servicing can include installation, replenishment, product changes, routine maintenance and technical support after a fault is reported through the agreed contact. Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. No response time is published. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status. It is not on every machine. Cleaning requirements are agreed for the chosen setup.

Do you hire or rent vending machines?

People searching for hire or rental usually want a machine without buying it. VendingNI’s core proposition is a fully managed service for qualifying locations: the site does not purchase the equipment, and stocking and routine support are handled as part of the managed arrangement.
