Buying, renting and managed vending are different

Buying gives you ownership of the equipment but normally leaves you responsible for stock, cashless systems, maintenance and repairs. Rental can reduce the upfront equipment cost, although the operator may still have substantial day-to-day responsibilities depending on the agreement.

VendingNI's core proposition is a fully managed service for qualifying locations. Rather than purchasing equipment, the site receives a suitable machine and VendingNI manages stocking and routine support.

Buy

Best suited to organisations that specifically want to own equipment and manage the vending operation themselves.

Rent or lease

May spread equipment cost but terms, responsibilities and servicing arrangements vary by supplier.

Fully managed

For qualifying sites, VendingNI supplies the machine and handles the ongoing vending operation.

Which route should you choose?

If your main objective is to provide refreshments without creating another operational task for your staff, a fully managed service is usually the most relevant option to discuss with us.
