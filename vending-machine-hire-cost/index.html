Vending Machine Hire Cost: What You Actually Pay For

There is no single price for hiring a vending machine. What you pay depends on the machine, the length of the agreement, and above all on what the agreement includes. One supplier's low weekly rate may cover only the equipment. Another's may include stocking, servicing and card payments. This guide explains the parts that make up a hire or rental cost, the questions to ask before you sign, and when a fully managed machine costs a qualifying site nothing at all.

VendingNI does not publish a hire tariff. For qualifying workplaces and venues across the UK (England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland), our route is a fully managed service. The machine is supplied, stocked and serviced, with no equipment to buy and no installation charge. More on that below. First, the cost of hiring.

Why there is no standard hire price

Prices shown online vary widely because they are rarely describing the same thing. The main things that move the cost are:

  • Machine type and size. A snack spiral machine, a chilled drinks machine, a combination machine and a coffee machine are different pieces of equipment. Size and refrigeration requirements both affect equipment pricing.
  • New or refurbished equipment. Age and condition affect the rate, and they also affect reliability and whether modern card readers are supported.
  • Payment technology. A card or contactless reader is extra hardware, and card transactions can carry processing fees. Ask who pays them.
  • Length of the agreement. The length of the term affects pricing, and some agreements charge for ending early.
  • What is included. Stock, refills, cleaning, servicing and repairs may be in the price, cost extra, or be left to you.
  • Your site. Delivery access, stairs or lifts, the position of the machine and the number of machines all matter.

That is why a headline weekly or monthly figure tells you little on its own. Two offers are only comparable once you know what each one includes.

If the answer to most of the right-hand column is "you do" or "extra", the headline hire rate is only part of the real cost.

The cost that does not appear on the invoice

Hiring a machine usually means someone on site takes on the running of it. That means ordering stock, filling the machine, handling unsold or out-of-date products, reporting faults and chasing repairs. This is time taken from people who have other jobs. When a vending machine costs the site nothing

For a qualifying location, a fully managed service removes the hire bill altogether. The machine stays part of the managed service, so there is no equipment to buy and no installation charge. For qualifying sites the service can include:

  • the machine, delivery and installation
  • stocking and refill visits, with product changes handled for you
  • routine servicing, maintenance and machine support
  • cashless and contactless payments, including debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, where the equipment and reader support them

Snack, drinks, combination and coffee machines can all be considered, and healthier options can be included where they suit the people using the machine. See the machines page for the formats. For offices that mainly want hot drinks, we can also discuss bean-to-cup office coffee machines, including rental and lease arrangements.

Which sites qualify?

A managed machine is not placed on request. Each site is assessed on expected usage, space, access and the products needed. As approximate network guidelines, not acceptance rules:

  • an office or workplace with around 40 or more regular users
  • a hotel with around 50 or more rooms
  • an apartment building with around 200 or more apartments
  • factories, gyms, schools, hospitals and transport sites with steady, recurring footfall

A smaller site may still be viable where it has multiple shifts, long opening hours or concentrated demand. A very quiet site, or one where servicing would be impractical, may not be. Managed vending is not automatically the cheapest option for every site. If you want to own the machine and choose your own supplier, hiring or buying may suit you better. Questions to ask any supplier before you sign

  1. What exactly does the weekly or monthly charge include?
  2. Is the equipment new or refurbished, and which model is it?
  3. Who buys the stock, and who fills the machine?
  4. Is there a card reader, and who pays the transaction fees?
  5. Are servicing, repairs and call-outs included?
  6. How long is the minimum term, and what does it cost to end early?
  7. What happens to the machine at the end of the agreement?
  8. Would a fully managed service be available for our site instead?

Vending machine hire cost: FAQs

How much does it cost to hire a vending machine in the UK?

It depends on the machine, the length of the agreement and what is included. Equipment, delivery, card payments, stock, refills and servicing can each be included or charged separately, so compare offers element by element rather than by the headline rate. VendingNI does not publish a hire tariff. For qualifying sites we provide a fully managed machine with no equipment purchase and no installation charge.

Is renting a vending machine worth it?

Renting can suit an organisation that wants a machine without buying one and is happy to manage stock and servicing, or pay for those separately. If nobody on site wants to run the machine, a fully managed service is usually the better question to ask, as long as the site qualifies.

Can I get a vending machine for free?

For qualifying locations, yes. With a fully managed service the machine is supplied, stocked and serviced, and the site does not buy the equipment or pay an installation charge. Every site is assessed individually, and qualification is not guaranteed.

Do you publish vending machine hire prices?

No. Our service for qualifying sites is fully managed rather than a priced hire. We would rather tell you honestly whether your site suits a managed machine than publish a figure that does not reflect your site.

Want to know whether your site qualifies?

Send us the location, approximate number of people and the vending you need, and we will tell you whether a fully managed machine is realistic.
