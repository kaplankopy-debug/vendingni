Vending for Dublin workplaces and venues

A well-placed vending machine gives employees, visitors or customers access to refreshments without the space and staffing requirements of a shop or canteen. For qualifying locations, VendingNI can provide the equipment and manage the operational side of the service.

We consider site usage, available space and preferred product range before recommending a machine. Cashless payment capability can help make the service practical for modern workplaces where fewer people routinely carry cash.
