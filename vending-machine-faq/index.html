Frequently asked questions

Eligibility and cost

Who manages my vending service?

VendingNI is your contact for arranging the service. For an accepted placement, the managed service can include equipment provision, installation, replenishment, maintenance and ongoing support. See our managed vending experience.

Can stock levels and sales be monitored remotely?

Compatible telemetry can report information such as stock position, transactions, product selections, alerts or machine status. It is not available on every installation, and the data visible depends on the machine, reader and service arrangement.

How do operators know when to restock?

Refill planning uses actual demand, machine capacity, operating hours and access. Where compatible monitoring is fitted, stock and transaction data can provide additional evidence for planning the next visit.

What happens if usage is lower than expected?

The product mix, refill pattern or continuing commercial suitability may need review. No single outcome applies to every placement; the agreed arrangement and actual usage determine the next step.

How many employees do you need for a free vending machine?

Approximately 40 regular workplace users is a typical network guideline, not a guarantee. Footfall, shifts, opening hours, access and likely sales are also assessed.

Can a smaller workplace qualify?

Sometimes. Strong customer traffic, multiple shifts or long operating hours may make a smaller headcount viable.

Do hotels and apartment buildings qualify?

Typical network indicators are approximately 50 rooms for a hotel and approximately 200 apartments for a residential development. Both are guidelines and every site requires assessment.

Can I get a vending machine for free?

Qualifying locations can use VendingNI's fully managed service without purchasing the machine. Eligibility depends on factors such as expected footfall, space, access and likely demand. See how the free managed service works.

Who stocks the vending machine?

For VendingNI managed sites, product replenishment is handled as part of the service rather than left to the customer.

Who repairs or services the machine?

Routine servicing and machine support are included for machines within our managed service. See repairs and servicing.

How often is a machine restocked?

There is no universal schedule. Frequency depends on sales, capacity, operating hours and access. Higher-use sites need more frequent replenishment, and compatible monitoring can assist planning.

What happens if the machine breaks?

The site reports the fault through the agreed support route so technical support can be arranged. Timing depends on the fault, location and access; there is no universal response-time guarantee.

Can the machines accept cards and Apple Pay?

Compatible vending equipment can support debit cards and mobile wallets such as Apple Pay and Google Pay. See our cashless vending page.

Can a machine take cash?

Some machines can be configured for cash as well as cashless payments. Available methods depend on the selected equipment and reader.

Can we choose what goes in the machine?

Product selection can be discussed and adapted around the location and likely users, subject to the machine configuration and practical stocking requirements.

Do you provide snack and drinks machines?

Yes. Options include snack machines, cold-drink machines and combination machines.

Do you provide coffee vending machines?

Hot-drink vending can be discussed as part of a workplace refreshment setup. See coffee vending machines.

Where do you provide vending services?

VendingNI's core service area is Northern Ireland, including Belfast, with service enquiries also handled in the Republic of Ireland including Dublin. See our Northern Ireland vending service and Ireland pages.

What information should I send with an enquiry?

Include the location, type of organisation, approximate number of people using the site, available machine space and whether you want snacks, drinks, coffee or a combination.

How much space and power are needed?

Dimensions vary by model. A commercial machine normally needs a suitable level position, service access and an appropriate nearby power supply. Exact requirements are confirmed before installation.

How long does installation take?

Timing varies with equipment availability, site access and readiness. A date is agreed only after suitability, placement and setup requirements are confirmed.

How do you decide whether a site qualifies?

The assessment considers population or footfall, operating hours, shifts, venue type, access, placement, expected usage and service logistics. See the qualification guide.

Send us the location, approximate footfall and what you want the vending service to provide. We can then assess the most suitable setup.
