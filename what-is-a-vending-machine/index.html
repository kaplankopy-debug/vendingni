What Is a Vending Machine and How Does It Work?

A vending machine is an automated retail unit that allows users to select and purchase products without a member of staff completing the transaction.

How vending machines work

The user chooses a product, pays using an accepted method and the machine releases the selected item. Modern machines can combine mechanical product delivery with electronic payment systems, telemetry and refrigerated storage.

Common types of vending machine

  • Snack vending machines
  • Cold drinks vending machines
  • Combination snack-and-drink machines
  • Coffee and hot-drink machines
  • Specialist vending equipment for particular products or locations

How do people pay?

Depending on the equipment, users may pay with coins, notes, debit or credit cards, or mobile wallets such as Apple Pay and Google Pay.

Who looks after the machine?

That depends on the ownership model. An owner-operated machine requires the owner to manage products, maintenance and payments. In a fully managed vending arrangement, a vending operator handles those responsibilities for the site.

Prefer a managed vending service?

If you are considering buying or managing a machine yourself, a fully managed service may remove the equipment, stocking and maintenance burden for qualifying sites.
