Why offices use managed vending

Office vending can provide refreshments throughout the working day, including outside normal café or catering hours. It is especially useful for workplaces where employees would otherwise need to leave the building to buy a drink or snack.

Product selection can be adjusted around employee preferences and usage, including confectionery, crisps, cold drinks and healthier choices.

What we consider before installation

We look at approximate staff numbers and visitor footfall, available machine space, access for deliveries, preferred products and likely usage patterns. This helps match the machine to the workplace rather than using a one-size-fits-all approach.
