How Much Does a Vending Machine Cost?

The total cost of vending is about more than the purchase price. Equipment type, cashless hardware, delivery, installation, stock, maintenance and ongoing management can all affect the real cost.

There is no single vending machine price

Machine pricing varies significantly by size, age, condition, manufacturer, refrigeration requirements, payment technology and whether the equipment is new or used. That is why a single headline figure is rarely useful without knowing the specification.

Costs to consider when buying

  • Machine purchase price
  • Delivery and positioning
  • Electrical or site preparation if required
  • Card reader or cashless payment hardware
  • Opening stock
  • Ongoing replenishment
  • Repairs and replacement parts
  • Cleaning and routine maintenance

New versus used equipment

Used machines may lower the initial purchase cost, but condition, parts availability, payment-system compatibility and energy efficiency become especially important. A cheaper machine can become expensive if it requires frequent repairs or cannot support the payment options users expect.
