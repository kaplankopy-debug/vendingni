What happens next?

Is the machine really free?

For qualifying locations, you do not buy the machine or pay an installation charge. The machine is supplied as part of the fully managed service.

Who stocks and maintains it?

Stocking, replenishment, routine servicing and machine support are handled through the managed vending service.

Which areas do you cover?

VendingNI assesses enquiries across Northern Ireland, including Belfast and surrounding commercial areas.

What do you need from me?

Your location, approximate staff or visitor numbers, opening hours and the type of vending you need are usually enough for an initial assessment.
