Frequently asked questions

Is there a cost to install a vending machine?

For qualifying locations, the machine and installation can be provided as part of the fully managed service without an equipment purchase.

What areas do you cover?

VendingNI focuses on Northern Ireland, including Belfast and locations across the province.

Do you handle maintenance and repairs?

Yes. Routine servicing, maintenance and machine support can be included as part of the managed service.

How often are machines restocked?

Restocking is planned around usage, footfall and the needs of each location.

What types of vending machines are available?

Options can include snack, cold drinks, combination and coffee vending machines, depending on the site and expected demand.
