How to send an enquiry

Use email. There is no public web form and no published telephone number on this site. A short message to info@vendingni.com is enough to start a commercial assessment for a workplace, venue or other organisation.

This page is for new managed-vending enquiries and questions about whether a site is suitable. It is not a consumer complaints desk for machines that VendingNI does not operate.

Email address

Send commercial vending enquiries to the address below. Include the site details listed on this page so the location can be assessed.

info@vendingni.com

What to include

  • Your name, organisation and contact email
  • Postcode, town or city and site type
  • Approximate staff, resident, room, member or student population
  • Daily footfall, opening hours and shift pattern where known
  • Preferred machine type and products
  • Proposed position, available space and site-access details
  • Any payment, accessibility or other operational requirements

Estimates are useful. Not every detail has to be known before you enquire. See how site qualification works or try the eligibility checker.

What happens after you email

VendingNI reviews the information, asks for anything missing, and assesses whether the expected demand, access and space are suitable for a fully managed machine. If the site looks viable, a machine type and service arrangement can be proposed. Placement, setup and stocking are agreed before installation.

There is no universal response-time promise on this page. Timing depends on the information supplied and whether a site visit or further detail is needed.

Who this contact page is for

It is intended for organisations considering on-site snacks and drinks without buying or running the machine themselves. Typical enquiry types include offices, factories, warehouses, gyms, hotels, hospitals, schools, care homes, shared workspaces, apartment buildings and transport sites. Suitability is confirmed per location, not by site type alone.

If you already know you want a free managed machine, start from the free managed vending guide. If you are comparing hire, purchase or a managed service, see the hire and rental page and the cost comparison tool.

Service area

Enquiries are assessed across Northern Ireland, including Belfast, and the Republic of Ireland, including Dublin. Coverage for an individual site is confirmed during assessment. Read the Northern Ireland and Ireland service pages for local context.

Contact questions

How do I contact VendingNI?

Email info@vendingni.com. That is the public enquiry address published on this site.

Is there a phone number or office address?

This website publishes email as the contact method for new enquiries. A phone number or visiting address is not listed here.

What happens after I send an email?

The site details are reviewed and, where needed, more information is requested. If the location looks suitable for a managed machine, a setup can be discussed. No single timescale applies to every enquiry.

Can I enquire about a site in Ireland?

Yes. VendingNI assesses commercial enquiries in Northern Ireland and the Republic of Ireland, subject to site suitability and service availability.

Send the site details

Email the location, approximate number of people using the premises and the type of vending you want. We can then assess whether a fully managed machine is suitable.
