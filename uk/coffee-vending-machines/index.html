Hot-drinks machines, without a cups-per-day catalogue

A coffee enquiry starts with the format and the people who will use it. No coffee model on this site is published with a cups-per-day rating. SandenVendo is the equipment family already named for vending; the daily figure is not. The decision uses the people in the building, the expected cups you can describe, and the peak.

Hot-drinks cabinet

The self-service cabinet makes coffee and any other hot drink agreed for that equipment. The menu depends on the equipment and the ingredient system. It is not published as one national list.

Bean-to-cup, if that is the comparison

Bean-to-cup grinds beans for a team and can be rental, lease or fully managed. That is a different machine. See office coffee machines.

SandenVendo equipment

VendingNI supplies SandenVendo equipment, with size and configuration selected for the location. No cups-per-day figure is published for it. Counter or floor space, and room to replenish ingredients, decide whether it fits.

How a UK workplace gets coffee vending

For a qualifying location there is no equipment purchase and no installation charge. The machine remains part of the service. The host organisation does not normally manage day-to-day ingredient runs. Service delivery is supported through an established vending network.

  • The machine is supplied and installed once a placement is agreed
  • The first fill, later replenishment and product changes are handled for the site
  • Routine servicing, maintenance and technical support sit with the managed service
  • Card, contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay depend on the machine and the reader

Cleaning requirements and the technical setup are agreed for the chosen equipment. The same managed model is used in England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Northern Ireland coffee searches can also start at coffee vending machines in Northern Ireland. The wider UK service is fully managed vending across the UK.

Who it suits, by site size and expected cups

About 40 regular users is a typical workplace starting guideline, subject to assessment. Expected cups can matter as much as headcount: a smaller team that each take several hot drinks can be a clearer case than a large payroll that brings coffee from home. Expected cups are your description of current demand. They are not a machine rating.

Users

Count people in the building on a normal day, not the number on the lease. Hybrid attendance changes this in offices.

Peak breaks

If everyone arrives in a short window, the machine has to cope with a queue. If drinks are spread across a shift, reliability matters as much as capacity.

Water, power and space

A hot-drinks machine needs a practical water supply and power where it will stand, and room to replenish ingredients without blocking a fire route.

Hot drinks rarely replace snacks and chilled drinks. Those can be separate machines or a combination machine beside the coffee cabinet. Factories often need drinks at shift changes. Shared offices need something that does not depend on one tenant’s kitchen.

Questions people ask

What does it cost?

There is no tariff on this page. For a qualifying location, a fully managed placement means no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. That result is not guaranteed for every enquiry. The hire cost guide and the cost comparison describe the shape of the cost — the machine, the stock and the staff time — without a price list.

Which coffee machine is the right question?

A large self-service break points to the hot-drinks cabinet on this page. Bean-to-cup in a staff room or reception points to office coffee machines. Say the workplace, how many people are in, the expected cups, the peak, and whether water and power are in the right place.

What does servicing include?

On a machine in the managed service, servicing can include installation, replenishment, product changes, routine maintenance and technical support after a fault is reported through the agreed contact. Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. No response time is published. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status. It is not on every machine. Cleaning requirements are agreed for the chosen setup.

Tell us about hot-drink demand

Email the workplace, roughly how many people are in on a normal day, and anything you know about expected cups, peak breaks, and water and power.
