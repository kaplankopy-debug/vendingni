Healthy vending machines

Healthy Vending Machines for UK Workplaces

Healthy vending machines for workplaces and venues across the UK, arranged as a fully managed service where the site qualifies. A dedicated healthy range is agreed with the site and placed in the machine. It can sit alongside a standard snack and drinks mix, or be the range the site asks for instead. VendingNI supplies the machine, installs it and keeps it stocked. It is not a cabinet offered for sale. The service model is a fully managed vending service. Machine formats are compared on the machines page.

What a healthy range in a managed machine means

A healthy range is a product selection agreed for that workplace or venue, not a published catalogue. Healthier snack and drink choices can be included where they suit the people using the machine. A dedicated healthy vending range is available. This page does not list brands, nutrition scores or certifications, because none are published for the range.

Agreed with the site

The organisation says what it will allow, and the range is chosen around the people who will use the machine. A school, hospital or care setting may already have its own rules. Those rules are part of the brief. They are not replaced by a national product list.

Alongside, or instead

Some sites keep a familiar snack and drinks selection and add healthier lines. Others ask for the dedicated range in place of the standard mix. There is no rule that every selection has to be a healthier line. Snack vending machines and drinks vending machines describe the standard packaged formats. A combination machine is the comparison when snacks and chilled drinks share one position.

Cashless payments

Card, contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay depend on the machine and the reader. Cash acceptance may be available on some equipment. See cashless vending machines for how payment is arranged on a managed placement.

How stocking and servicing work

For a qualifying location there is no equipment purchase and no installation charge. The machine remains part of the service. The host organisation does not normally buy the stock or chase faults. The same questions are used for a site in England, Scotland, Wales or Northern Ireland, and coverage in the Republic of Ireland is assessed on the same managed model. UK coverage is vending machine UK.

  • The machine is supplied and installed once a placement is agreed
  • The first fill, later replenishment and product changes are handled for the site
  • Routine servicing, maintenance and technical support sit with the managed service
  • The range can be reviewed once it is clear what people actually buy

Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. No response time is published. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status. It is not on every machine. Deciding how much of the cabinet should be a healthier mix, before you enquire, is covered in choosing a healthy workplace vending mix.

Which sites a healthy range suits

The range can be considered wherever a managed machine is being assessed. About 40 regular users is a typical workplace starting guideline, subject to assessment. It is not a rule, and it is not a promise that every enquiry is accepted. Schools, hospitals and care homes are not judged as if they were an office of 40 staff. Use the people in the building, the hours, and the site’s own product rules.

Offices

A staff room or break area where people want a choice that is not only confectionery. Hybrid attendance means the people actually in the building matter more than the payroll.

Gyms

Members often want drinks and a snack after training. A healthier mix can be agreed for that demand. It is still a managed machine, not a staffed counter.

Schools

Products and who may use the machine follow the institution’s rules. The range is discussed with the site. Nothing on this page overrides that.

Hospitals

Staff and visitor areas can be assessed where the hospital has approved the placement and the products. Long opening hours are part of the brief.

Care homes

A machine for a staff room, reception or a suitable shared area. Say who is allowed to use it, and what the home will allow it to sell.

Factories

Shift sites can ask for a healthier mix in the break area, including outside canteen hours. Workforce per shift and access for replenishment are part of the assessment.

Questions people ask

What does it cost?

There is no tariff on this page. For a qualifying location, a fully managed placement means no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. That result is not guaranteed for every enquiry. The hire cost guide and the cost comparison describe the shape of the cost — the machine, the stock and the staff time — without a price list.

What is in the healthy range?

The range is agreed with the site. There is no published product list, brand list or nutrition standard. A dedicated healthy vending range is available, and healthier snack and drink choices can be included where they suit the people using the machine. A school, hospital, care home or other site may also set its own rules about what can be sold.

Can it replace the usual snacks and drinks?

It can sit alongside a standard snack and drinks mix, or the site can ask for the dedicated range instead. There is no rule that every selection has to be a healthier line. The mix follows the people using the machine and what the site will allow.

What does servicing include?

On a machine in the managed service, servicing can include installation, replenishment, product changes, routine maintenance and technical support after a fault is reported through the agreed contact. Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. No response time is published. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status. It is not on every machine. Cleaning requirements are agreed for the chosen setup.
