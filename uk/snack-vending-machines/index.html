What a UK snack placement is built from

A snack enquiry is about the people who will buy packaged food, and the space the cabinet can stand in. No packs-per-day figure is published, so the choice uses footfall, the break pattern and the product mix you can describe.

How a UK workplace gets snack vending

For a qualifying location there is no equipment purchase and no installation charge. The machine remains part of the service. The host organisation does not normally buy the stock or chase faults. Service delivery is supported through an established vending network.

  • The machine is supplied and installed once a placement is agreed
  • The first fill, later replenishment and product changes are handled for the site
  • Routine servicing, maintenance and technical support sit with the managed service
  • Card, contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay depend on the machine and the reader

The same questions are used for a workplace in England, Scotland, Wales or Northern Ireland: who is actually in, the hours, the space and whether the position can be replenished. The service model is fully managed vending. UK coverage is vending machine UK. Chilled drinks that need their own cabinet are on drinks vending machines for UK workplaces.

Which UK sites a snack machine suits

Snack machines suit workplaces where people repeatedly buy packaged food. About 40 regular users is a typical workplace starting guideline, subject to assessment. It is not a rule, and it is not a promise that every enquiry is accepted.

People in the building

Count people on site on a normal day, not the number on the payroll. Hybrid attendance can make the real population smaller than the headcount. Visitor footfall and long opening hours can still make a smaller site worth assessing.

One break area

A single cabinet for snacks alone suits a site whose demand is packaged food. Where snacks and chilled drinks must share one position, a combination machine is the comparison. An office of about 80 people is a selection example on the machines page, not an acceptance threshold.

Refill access

The position needs room to restock, and delivery access is part of the assessment.

Questions people ask

What does it cost?

There is no tariff on this page. For a qualifying location, a fully managed placement means no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. That result is not guaranteed for every enquiry. The hire cost guide and the cost comparison describe the shape of the cost — the machine, the stock and the staff time — without a price list.

Is this a snack machine to buy?

No. This page describes a fully managed snack placement for a qualifying UK workplace. The organisation does not buy the machine from a price list here. Where the site qualifies, supply, installation, stocking and routine support sit with the service, and the machine remains part of that service. A site that wants to own the equipment should say so in the enquiry.

What does servicing include?

On a machine in the managed service, servicing can include installation, replenishment, product changes, routine maintenance and technical support after a fault is reported through the agreed contact. Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. No response time is published. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status. It is not on every machine. Cleaning requirements are agreed for the chosen setup.

Tell us about snack demand

Email the workplace, roughly how many people are in on a normal day, the hours, and whether the machine is for snacks alone or snacks with cold drinks.
