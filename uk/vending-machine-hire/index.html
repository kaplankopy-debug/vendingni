Vending machine rental compared with a fully managed machine

Vending machine rental, like hire, means having a machine on site for a period without buying it. A lease is commonly finance over an agreed term. On a typical hire, rental or lease, someone still fills the machine and arranges help when it fails. Buying means the organisation pays for the equipment and then arranges delivery, stock and repairs. How much a vending machine costs explains ownership costs.

For a qualifying location the fully managed machine is different. There is no equipment purchase and no installation charge. The machine stays part of the managed service. Planned replenishment, product changes, routine servicing, maintenance and machine support are handled for the site. The wider service is fully managed vending.

Which UK setup should we ask for?

Choose the format first: snack, cold drinks, combination or coffee. Then say whether you want the fully managed service or you are comparing it with hire, rental or lease. Include the nation, the town, how many people use the site, and the products.

What does servicing include?

On a machine in the managed service, servicing can include installation, replenishment, product changes, routine maintenance and technical support after a fault is reported through the agreed contact. Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status; we confirm whether it is fitted when the machine is agreed. Cleaning requirements are agreed for the chosen setup.
