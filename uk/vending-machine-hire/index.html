Hire, rental, lease, and a fully managed service

In general, hire means paying to have a machine on site for a period without owning it at the end. Rental is often the same idea. A lease is commonly finance over an agreed term. The labels overlap, and this page does not describe any particular provider’s contract. On a typical hire or rental, someone still fills the machine and arranges help when it fails. On a lease, the site often still carries that work. Buying means the organisation pays for the equipment and then arranges delivery, stock and repairs. How much a vending machine costs explains ownership costs. It is not a hire tariff.

For a qualifying location there is no equipment purchase and no installation charge. The machine stays part of the managed service. Planned replenishment, product changes, routine servicing, maintenance and machine support are handled for the site. Service delivery is supported through an established vending network. The wider service is fully managed vending. Northern Ireland hire searches can also start at hire and rental in Northern Ireland.

Cashless payments, including debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, can be part of a managed machine when the equipment and reader support them. Monitoring can be included on suitable equipment. Neither is automatic on a generic hire. The managed route is the better question when nobody on site wants to own the stock or arrange repairs. It is the wrong question if the organisation wants to own the asset, or if usage, access or the only available position would make replenishment unrealistic.

Who it suits across the UK, by site size

Enquiries are taken for qualifying workplaces and venues in England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Every site is assessed on its own. About 40 regular users is a typical workplace starting guideline.

Below the usual workplace guideline

About 40 regular workplace users is a starting point, not a rule. A smaller site can still be assessed when footfall, long opening hours or shift demand creates steady use. Hybrid attendance means the number of people actually in the building matters more than the payroll.

Larger workplaces

Offices, factories whose canteen closes between shifts, and hotels with demand outside restaurant hours are typical. Hotel guidance starts at about 50 rooms and apartment guidance at about 200 apartments. Questions people ask

What does it cost?

There is no tariff on this page. For a qualifying location, a fully managed placement means no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. That result is not guaranteed for every enquiry. The hire cost guide and the cost comparison describe the shape of the cost — the machine, the stock and the staff time — without a price list.

Which UK setup should we ask for?

Choose the format first: snack, cold drinks, combination or coffee. Then say whether you want the fully managed service or you are comparing it with hire, rental or lease. Include the nation, the town, how many people use the site, and the products.

What does servicing include?

On a machine in the managed service, servicing can include installation, replenishment, product changes, routine maintenance and technical support after a fault is reported through the agreed contact. Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. No response time is published. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status. It is not on every machine. Cleaning requirements are agreed for the chosen setup.
