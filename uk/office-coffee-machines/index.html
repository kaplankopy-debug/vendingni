Bean-to-cup, rental and managed

Office Coffee Machines for Businesses Across the UK

Bean-to-cup coffee for offices across the UK. A qualifying workplace can take a fully managed machine, with no equipment to buy and no installation charge. Rental and lease are also offered, without a published price.

Bean-to-cup machines, with no model list

This page does not name brands or models, and it does not publish a cups-per-day figure. The opening decision is the format: beans ground for each drink, in a staff room, reception or smaller office.

Bean-to-cup

The machine grinds beans for the drink. The menu depends on the selected equipment and ingredient system, agreed for that machine rather than published as one national list. It is a workplace machine, not a café bar and not a domestic machine sold for a business to own.

Hot-drinks cabinet

A coffee vending machine is a self-service cabinet loaded with ingredients. It suits a larger break. See coffee vending machines across the UK.

What is not listed

No brand, model or daily cup rating is published here. Capacity is discussed from how many people are in and how they break, not from a specification table.

Fully managed, rental, or lease

The free option is fully managed vending, and only for a qualifying workplace. There is no equipment purchase and no installation charge. The machine remains part of the managed service. Restocking and servicing are included for the machines VendingNI manages. The organisation does not normally run day-to-day restocking. Service delivery is supported through an established vending network.

Coffee machine rental and coffee machine lease are both offered where a business wants the machine without buying it. People also say coffee machine hire for one of those arrangements. No price and no contract length are published, so none are stated here. A rental or a lease is not guaranteed for every enquiry, and neither is the free option. Only the managed arrangement is free, and only where the workplace qualifies. A workplace that does not qualify can still ask about rental or lease. For how vending hire compares more generally, see vending machine hire across the UK.

Which offices it suits, by size

Bean-to-cup suits a team that wants coffee from beans in a staff room, reception or smaller office. A large self-service break may be better as a hot-drinks cabinet. Assessment looks at staff numbers, visitor footfall, space, delivery access, products, usage, hybrid attendance and break patterns.

Below the usual workplace guideline

About 40 regular workplace users is a starting point, not a rule. A smaller site can still be assessed when footfall, long opening hours or shift demand creates steady use. Hybrid attendance means the number of people actually in the building matters more than the payroll.

Around 40 regular users

That is the typical workplace guideline used across this site. It is an assessment starting point. Space, delivery access, products and break patterns are still part of the decision.

Larger workplaces

An office of about 80 people is used on the machines page as a selection example, not a promise of acceptance. A combination machine may cover snacks and chilled drinks in one break area, with coffee assessed separately if hot drinks are the real demand.

The same questions apply to a team room in a factory or warehouse when the demand is bean-to-cup for that team, rather than a cabinet on the shop floor. Snack and cold-drink machines are a separate choice, covered under office vending machines.

Questions people ask

What does it cost?

There is no tariff on this page. For a qualifying location, a fully managed placement means no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. That result is not guaranteed for every enquiry. The hire cost guide and the cost comparison describe the shape of the cost — the machine, the stock and the staff time — without a price list. Rental and lease have no published price or term. Only the fully managed arrangement, for a qualifying workplace, is the free option.

Should we ask for bean-to-cup, rental, or the free managed option?

Ask for bean-to-cup when the complaint is the coffee in a team room. Ask for the free fully managed option if the workplace may qualify and nobody wants to buy the machine. Ask about rental or lease if you want the machine without buying it and the free option is not the question. The drinks are agreed for the machine that is chosen.

What does servicing include?

On a machine in the managed service, servicing can include installation, replenishment, product changes, routine maintenance and technical support after a fault is reported through the agreed contact. Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. No response time is published. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status. It is not on every machine. Cleaning requirements are agreed for the chosen setup. That servicing description is for machines VendingNI manages. Rental and lease inclusions are not published.

Do you supply bean-to-cup machines?

Yes. Managed bean-to-cup office coffee machines are supplied, including a bean-to-cup coffee machine for office use. The drinks depend on the selected equipment and ingredient system. They are agreed for the machine that is chosen, rather than published as one national list. This page does not name brands or models.
