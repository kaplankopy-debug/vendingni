Office coffee machines for UK businesses are bean-to-cup machines for a staff room, reception or smaller office, and the wider question of how a workplace gets coffee without running a café. The opening decision is the format, then whether the arrangement is rental, lease or fully managed.

How it works

For a managed machine the route is the one already used across the site.

  • Enquiry. Email the workplace, how many people are in on a normal day, and whether you want bean-to-cup, a hot drinks cabinet, rental, lease or the fully managed option.
  • Site assessment. The assessment looks at staff numbers, visitor footfall, space, delivery access, water, power, products, hybrid attendance and break patterns.
  • Installation. Once a placement is agreed, the machine is supplied and installed.
  • Servicing and replenishment. On a managed machine, the first fill, later replenishment, product changes, routine servicing and technical support sit with the managed service.

Every site is assessed before a machine is placed. Low usage, awkward access or a position that cannot be replenished can mean a site is not suitable.

Which offices it suits, by size

Bean-to-cup suits a team that wants coffee from beans in a staff room, reception or smaller office. A large self-service break may be better as a hot-drinks cabinet. About 40 regular users is a typical workplace starting guideline, and it is an assessment starting point.

Questions people ask

What does it cost?

For a qualifying workplace, a fully managed placement means no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. Should we ask for bean-to-cup, rental, or the free managed option?

Ask for bean-to-cup when the team wants coffee from beans in a staff room or reception. Ask for the free fully managed option if the workplace may qualify and nobody wants to buy the machine. Ask about rental or lease if you want the machine without buying it and the free option is not the question. The drinks are agreed for the machine that is chosen.

What does servicing include?

On a machine in the managed service, servicing can include installation, replenishment, product changes, routine maintenance and technical support after a fault is reported through the agreed contact. Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status, and that is confirmed with the machine. Cleaning requirements are agreed for the chosen setup. That description is for machines VendingNI manages. Rental and lease terms depend on the site and the machine.

Do you supply bean-to-cup machines?

Yes. Managed bean-to-cup office coffee machines are supplied, including a bean-to-cup coffee machine for office use. The drinks depend on the selected equipment and ingredient system. They are agreed for the machine that is chosen.

Is coffee machine rental the same as a coffee machine lease?

Both mean using a machine without buying it. VendingNI offers each. Rental and lease terms depend on the site and the machine; get in touch and we'll talk you through the options. Neither is the free option. Only a fully managed placement, where the workplace qualifies, has no equipment purchase and no installation charge.
