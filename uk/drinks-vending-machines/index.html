A cold-drinks cabinet, without a bottles-per-day rating

No drinks model on this site is published with a bottles-per-day figure. The useful question is whether cold drinks need their own cabinet, and which products the people on site actually take.

Cold-drink cabinet

A dedicated drinks machine holds water, soft drinks, energy drinks and other chilled beverages, adjusted to the site. It suits workplaces where drink demand is stronger than snack demand, including shift sites and busy staff areas.

How a UK site gets a drinks machine

For a qualifying location the drinks machine is supplied, filled and supported. There is no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. Service delivery is supported through an established vending network.

  • The machine is supplied and installed once a placement is agreed
  • The first fill, later replenishment and product changes are handled for the site
  • Routine servicing, maintenance and technical support sit with the managed service
  • Card, contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay depend on the machine and the reader

Refill frequency follows sales, season, capacity and opening hours. England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland are assessed on those same site questions. The service model is fully managed vending. UK coverage is vending machine UK.

Who a drinks machine suits

The test is recurring cold-drink demand. About 40 regular workplace users is the usual starting guideline. Hotels are often discussed from about 50 rooms. Those figures are guidelines for assessment, not a guarantee.

A normal working day

Staff who are in the building, plus visitors, matter more than a lease schedule. A smaller office can still be assessed when footfall or long hours create steady drink sales. Hybrid attendance changes the number of people actually on site.

Peaks and shifts

If drinks are taken in a short break, the cabinet has to cope with a queue. If they are spread across a shift, reliability and refill access matter as much as capacity. Membership or headcount on paper is a weak guide for gyms and factories.

Water, soft drinks, energy drinks

The range is adjusted to the site. Healthier drink choices can be included where they suit the people using the machine. A dedicated healthy vending range is available, and there is no published national drinks list.

Questions people ask

What does it cost?

There is no tariff on this page. For a qualifying location, a fully managed placement means no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. That result is not guaranteed for every enquiry. The hire cost guide and the cost comparison describe the shape of the cost — the machine, the stock and the staff time — without a price list.

Cold drinks, snacks, or hot drinks?

Ask for a drinks machine when the queue is for cold drinks. Ask for a combination machine when snacks and drinks must share one position. Ask for coffee separately when the demand is hot drinks. Payment methods — debit cards, contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay — depend on the reader fitted to that machine.

What does servicing include?

On a machine in the managed service, servicing can include installation, replenishment, product changes, routine maintenance and technical support after a fault is reported through the agreed contact. Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. No response time is published. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status. It is not on every machine. Cleaning requirements are agreed for the chosen setup.
