Cashless vending machines for workplaces across the UK. Card and mobile-wallet payments sit on a compatible reader, fitted to a host machine inside a fully managed placement where the site qualifies. Cashless is a reader on a managed machine

No payment-reader brand and no transaction-per-day figure are published. The reader is confirmed when the host machine is chosen. Staff who do not carry coins are a reason to ask for that reader, not a reason to skip the site assessment.

The host machine

Cashless applies to a snack, cold-drink, combination or coffee machine. It is not a separate cabinet. UK snack and drinks placements are snack vending machines for UK workplaces and drinks vending machines for UK workplaces.

SandenVendo equipment

VendingNI supplies SandenVendo equipment. Size and configuration follow the space, the expected usage and the products.

Payments that can be requested

Compatible equipment can take debit cards, credit cards, contactless payments, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Cash acceptance can remain on some configurations.

How a UK workplace gets cashless vending

The managed route is a qualifying placement of a host machine with a compatible reader. For that location there is no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. Service delivery is supported through an established vending network.

  • The machine is supplied and installed once a placement is agreed
  • The first fill, later replenishment and product changes are handled for the site
  • Routine servicing, maintenance and technical support sit with the managed service
  • Card, contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay depend on the machine and the reader

Some compatible systems can send transaction or machine-status data to help plan visits. That depends on the reader, the machine, connectivity and the installation. See the network experience note on monitoring. The service model is fully managed vending. UK coverage is vending machine UK. A reader can be requested on a placement in England, Scotland, Wales or Northern Ireland.

Where cashless payments help

Cashless is most useful where people do not carry coins: offices, gyms, hotels, hospitals, apartment buildings and transport sites. The site-size test is still the host location. About 40 regular users is the usual workplace guideline. Hotels from about 50 rooms, and apartment buildings from about 200 apartments, are the other published guidelines. They are not guarantees.

Offices and hybrid workplaces

People on a normal day, not the payroll, decide whether a reader is worth fitting. Space, delivery access and a position that can be replenished are still part of the assessment.

Visitors and residents

Hotel guests and apartment residents often have a card and no coins. Travellers on a transport site are judged on visits across the day. A reader does not replace that footfall check.

Long hours

Gyms, hospitals and shift sites are judged on visits across the day, including evenings.

Questions people ask

What does it cost?

There is no tariff on this page. For a qualifying location, a fully managed placement means no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. That result is not guaranteed for every enquiry. The hire cost guide and the cost comparison describe the shape of the cost — the machine, the stock and the staff time — without a price list.

Which payments can a UK workplace ask for?

Say which payments matter: card, contactless, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or cash as well. Then say the workplace, the number of users, the products and the machine type. Compatible equipment can take debit cards, credit cards, contactless payments, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Not every method is available on every machine. The reader is agreed with the machine. It is not chosen from a published hardware list.

What does servicing include?

On a machine in the managed service, servicing can include installation, replenishment, product changes, routine maintenance and technical support after a fault is reported through the agreed contact. Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. No response time is published. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status. It is not on every machine. Cleaning requirements are agreed for the chosen setup.
