Choosing the best vending machine for your site

A combination machine is often useful when snacks and cold drinks are needed but space is limited. Higher-volume factories and workplaces may benefit from separate snack and drink machines for greater capacity.

Office, hotel and gym vending

Offices may prioritise coffee alongside snacks, hotels often need mixed products for out-of-hours access, and gyms commonly emphasise cold drinks, convenient products and cashless payments.

How many vending machines do I need?

One machine may suit moderate mixed demand. Multiple machines can provide more capacity and product choice for high-footfall, multi-shift or transport environments. Final selection depends on site assessment, capacity, access and expected demand.
