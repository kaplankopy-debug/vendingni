Belfast vending FAQs

Do you provide vending machines in Belfast?

Yes. VendingNI supplies a fully managed vending machine service for qualifying commercial workplaces and venues in Belfast. Every enquiry starts with a site assessment.

Is a vending machine in Belfast supplied as a fully managed service?

For qualifying sites, yes. VendingNI provides the machine, arranges installation, manages stocking and handles routine support. The host organisation does not normally run day-to-day vending itself.

Which Belfast sites can enquire?

Commercial offices, factories, warehouses, hotels, gyms and other workplaces or venues with regular demand. Suitability depends on users, hours, access, space and likely use — not on the Belfast postcode alone.

Is vending machine hire the same as a managed machine in Belfast?

No. Hire or rental is usually an equipment arrangement. VendingNI's usual model is a fully managed service for qualifying locations rather than a published hire tariff.
