Vending solutions for workplaces and venues

Snack vending machines

Snack vending provides a convenient self-service refreshment option for offices, factories, leisure facilities and other busy locations.

Cold drink vending

Chilled drinks vending can provide convenient access to refreshments throughout the working day and outside normal catering hours.

Combination vending

Combination machines allow snacks and chilled drinks to be offered through a single vending unit where space or demand suits a combined setup.

Modern cashless vending

Modern vending equipment can support convenient cashless payment options, including debit cards and compatible mobile payment systems. This makes vending easier to use in workplaces and venues where customers increasingly expect contactless payment.

The appropriate equipment depends on factors such as available space, expected footfall, product demand and the type of organisation using the machine.

Where vending machines work well

Managed and commercial vending solutions can work particularly well in offices, manufacturing facilities, warehouses, gyms, hotels, residential buildings and other locations where people need convenient access to refreshments.

Every location is different, so machine size, product selection and service arrangements should be matched to expected demand and the needs of the site.
