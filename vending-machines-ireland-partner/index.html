Equipment the partnership talks about

Vending-Machines.ie supplies commercial vending equipment for organisations across Ireland, including workplaces, hospitality, leisure and other commercial environments. This page does not add model names or cups-per-day figures that the partnership has not published.

Snack, drinks and combination

Snack machines, beverage machines and combination machines are part of the range described for workplaces, hospitality and leisure. The product mix still has to match the site.

Coffee and other equipment

Coffee machines and other specialist vending equipment are named as available solutions. No brand list or daily capacity is published on this page.

Cashless

Modern equipment can support debit cards and compatible mobile payments. The reader depends on the machine that is chosen.

How the collaboration works

VendingNI provides vending solutions across Northern Ireland and works with Vending-Machines.ie in connection with vending requirements across the Republic of Ireland. Together, the two businesses can support organisations seeking modern vending equipment and practical ongoing service across the island of Ireland.

The customer route is still an assessed placement rather than a shop of machines to buy. You can read more about the company and its equipment at Vending-Machines.ie. The service page for the Republic is vending machines in Ireland, and Dublin is on Dublin vending.

  • Snack, drinks, combination and cashless formats are the solutions described for the collaboration
  • Machine size, product selection and service arrangements are matched to the site
  • No price list, fleet size or response time is published for the partnership on this page

Which Irish sites it suits, by size

Offices, manufacturing, warehouses, gyms, hotels, residential buildings and other locations with regular refreshment demand can be described. The same published guidelines apply: about 40 regular workplace users, about 50 hotel rooms, and about 200 apartments, each subject to assessment.

Below the usual workplace guideline

About 40 regular workplace users is a starting point, not a rule. A smaller site can still be assessed when footfall, long opening hours or shift demand creates steady use. Hybrid attendance means the number of people actually in the building matters more than the payroll.

Around 40 regular users

That is the typical workplace guideline used across this site. It is an assessment starting point. Space, delivery access, products and break patterns are still part of the decision.

Larger workplaces

An office of about 80 people is used on the machines page as a selection example, not a promise of acceptance. A combination machine may cover snacks and chilled drinks in one break area, with coffee assessed separately if hot drinks are the real demand.

Every location is different. Expected demand, space and access decide whether a managed arrangement is practical.

Questions people ask

What does it cost?

There is no tariff on this page. For a qualifying location, a fully managed placement means no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. That result is not guaranteed for every enquiry. The hire cost guide and the cost comparison describe the shape of the cost — the machine, the stock and the staff time — without a price list. The partnership page does not publish equipment prices or a hire tariff.

Which setup should an Ireland enquiry ask for?

Say the location, how many people use the premises, and whether you need snacks, cold drinks, a combination cabinet, coffee, or cashless payments. The enquiry is assessed. It is not an instant equipment order.

What does servicing include?

On a machine in the managed service, servicing can include installation, replenishment, product changes, routine maintenance and technical support after a fault is reported through the agreed contact. Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. No response time is published. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status. It is not on every machine. Cleaning requirements are agreed for the chosen setup.

Looking for a vending machine in Ireland?

Email the location, approximate number of people using the premises, and the type of vending required.
