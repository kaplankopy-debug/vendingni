Which vending machine do I need?

Consider the regular site population, daily footfall, busy periods, whether access is needed around the clock, available floor space, food-versus-drink demand, coffee demand and payment preferences. Indoor or exposed placement and the capacity needed between refill visits also affect the recommendation.

Example selection scenarios

  • Office with 80 staff: a combination machine may cover snacks and chilled drinks efficiently; coffee can be assessed separately.
  • Factory with three shifts: higher-capacity snack and drink provision may suit demand outside canteen hours.
  • Hotel with 70 rooms: a cashless combination machine near reception can support late-arriving guests.
  • Gym with strong evening footfall: a drinks-led or combination setup may suit hydration and packaged snack demand.
  • Apartment building with 250 units: a secure common-area combination machine can provide 24/7 convenience.

These are selection examples, not eligibility guarantees. A site assessment is still required.
