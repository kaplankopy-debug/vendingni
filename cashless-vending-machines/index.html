Cashless vending

Cashless Vending Machines in Northern Ireland

Card and mobile-wallet payments on compatible vending machines in Northern Ireland. Cashless is a reader on a host machine, supplied inside a fully managed placement where the site qualifies.

Cashless is fitted to a machine, not sold as its own model

No payment-reader brand or transaction-per-day figure is published. The named machines are the host equipment. The reader is confirmed when that machine is chosen.

Host machine

Cashless applies to snack, cold-drink, combination and coffee machines. It is not a separate cabinet.

SandenVendo equipment

VendingNI supplies SandenVendo equipment. Size and configuration follow the space, the expected usage and the products. No cups-per-day rating is published for it.

Payments that can be requested

Compatible equipment can take debit cards, credit cards, contactless payments, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Cash acceptance can remain on some configurations. Not every method is available on every machine.

How you get cashless vending

The managed route is a qualifying placement of a host machine with a compatible reader. You do not buy a payment terminal from a tariff on this page.

  • The machine is supplied and installed once a placement is agreed
  • The first fill, later replenishment and product changes are handled for the site
  • Routine servicing, maintenance and technical support sit with the managed service
  • Card, contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay depend on the machine and the reader

Some compatible systems can send transaction or machine-status data to help plan visits. That depends on the reader, the machine, connectivity and the installation. See the network experience note on monitoring. Coverage includes Belfast, Antrim, Lisburn, Dungannon, Mallusk and other Northern Ireland locations.

Who cashless vending suits, by site size

Cashless is most useful where people do not carry coins: offices, gyms, hotels, hospitals, apartment buildings and transport sites. The site-size test is still the host location, not the reader.

Workplaces

About 40 regular users is the usual starting guideline. Staff who do not carry cash are a reason to ask for a reader, not a reason to skip the headcount and access check.

Visitor sites

Hotels from about 50 rooms, and apartment buildings from about 200 apartments, are the published guidelines. Travellers and residents often have a card and no coins.

Long hours

Gyms, hospitals and transport sites are judged on visits across the day, including evenings. A reader does not change the need for a position that can be replenished.

Questions people ask

What does it cost?

There is no tariff on this page. For a qualifying location, a fully managed placement means no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. That result is not guaranteed for every enquiry. The hire cost guide and the cost comparison describe the shape of the cost — the machine, the stock and the staff time — without a price list.

Which cashless setup should we ask for?

Say which payments matter: card, contactless, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or cash as well. Then say the site, the number of users, the products and the machine type. The reader is agreed with the machine. It is not chosen from a published hardware list.

What does servicing include?

On a machine in the managed service, servicing can include installation, replenishment, product changes, routine maintenance and technical support after a fault is reported through the agreed contact. Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. No response time is published. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status. It is not on every machine. Cleaning requirements are agreed for the chosen setup.
