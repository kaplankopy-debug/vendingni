Combination Snack & Drink Vending Machines

One machine, broader product choice

Combination vending can be a practical alternative to installing separate snack and drinks machines. The exact configuration depends on the equipment and the types of products required.

Good fit for many workplaces

Offices, factories, gyms, hotels and shared workplaces can all benefit where expected demand justifies a managed machine but floor space does not require multiple units.

Managed stocking and support

Who combination vending suits

A combination machine serves packaged snacks and chilled drinks from one unit. It is useful where demand is mixed but floor space or usage does not justify separate machines.

Products, payments and stocking

The product balance can reflect local demand and be reviewed as usage becomes clear. Suitable readers may support cash, cards, contactless payments and mobile wallets. Replenishment depends on sales and capacity.

Service and alternatives

Managed service can include provision, installation, stocking and support. Higher-volume sites may benefit from separate snack and drinks machines; coffee demand should be assessed separately.
