Combination vending machines

Combination Snack & Drink Vending Machines

One cabinet for packaged snacks and chilled drinks, for sites that have mixed demand and limited floor space. Qualifying locations can take it as a fully managed service.

The combination format, and the named example

Combination machines are chosen because two product groups share one footprint. This site does not publish a vend-per-day figure for that format. It does name the equipment family and one example.

Sanden Vendo G-Snack

The Sanden Vendo G-Snack is the named snack and combination example. The overview describes the format. It does not publish a daily vend figure.

SandenVendo equipment

VendingNI supplies SandenVendo equipment. Size and configuration follow the space, the expected usage and the products. No cups-per-day rating is published for it.

What shares the cabinet

Snacks and chilled drinks are the two groups. Coffee is assessed separately. If either snacks or drinks dominate, compare a snack machine or a drinks machine on its own.

How you get a combination machine

On a qualifying site the combination machine is provided and then stocked and supported. The product balance can be reviewed once it is clear what people buy.

  • The machine is supplied and installed once a placement is agreed
  • The first fill, later replenishment and product changes are handled for the site
  • Routine servicing, maintenance and technical support sit with the managed service
  • Card, contactless, Apple Pay and Google Pay depend on the machine and the reader

Suitable readers may support cash, cards, contactless payments and mobile wallets. Not every method is fitted to every machine. See cashless vending and the machine comparison.

Who a combination machine suits, by site size

A combination machine is the practical choice when demand is mixed and the break area does not justify two cabinets. Higher-volume sites may still be better with separate snack and drinks machines.

Below the usual workplace guideline

About 40 regular workplace users is a starting point, not a rule. A smaller site can still be assessed when footfall, long opening hours or shift demand creates steady use. Hybrid attendance means the number of people actually in the building matters more than the payroll.

Around 40 regular users

That is the typical workplace guideline used across this site. It is an assessment starting point. Space, delivery access, products and break patterns are still part of the decision.

Larger workplaces

An office of about 80 people is used on the machines page as a selection example, not a promise of acceptance. A combination machine may cover snacks and chilled drinks in one break area, with coffee assessed separately if hot drinks are the real demand.

The machines page uses an office of about 80 people, a hotel of about 70 rooms and an apartment building of about 250 units as selection examples for this kind of mixed demand. They are examples, not eligibility rules. Hotel guidance elsewhere on the site starts at about 50 rooms, and apartment guidance at about 200 apartments.

Questions people ask

What does it cost?

There is no tariff on this page. For a qualifying location, a fully managed placement means no equipment purchase and no installation charge, and the machine remains part of the service. That result is not guaranteed for every enquiry. The hire cost guide and the cost comparison describe the shape of the cost — the machine, the stock and the staff time — without a price list.

Which setup should we ask for?

Ask for a combination machine when snacks and cold drinks are both needed and there is room for one cabinet. Ask for two machines when volume or a long run of one product would empty a shared cabinet too quickly. Tell us the population or footfall, the hours, the product mix, the floor space and how people will pay.

What does servicing include?

On a machine in the managed service, servicing can include installation, replenishment, product changes, routine maintenance and technical support after a fault is reported through the agreed contact. Refill timing follows usage, capacity, opening hours and access. No response time is published. Where monitoring is fitted, it can show stock, sales or machine status. It is not on every machine. Cleaning requirements are agreed for the chosen setup.

Tell us about the break area

Email the location, how many people use it, the hours, and whether the space can take one cabinet or two.
