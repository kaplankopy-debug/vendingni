Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This policy explains how VendingNI handles personal information when you visit this website or contact us about vending services.

Information we collect

We may collect information you choose to provide when you contact us, including your name, email address, telephone number, organisation, site address, approximate number of people using the site, and details of the vending service you are interested in.

We may also collect limited technical and usage information about visits to the website, such as pages viewed, device or browser information, referral source and general interaction data, through website analytics and similar technologies.

How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and assess whether a workplace or venue may be suitable for a vending service.
  • To pass relevant enquiry details to an appropriate vending service provider or partner where this is necessary to respond to or fulfil your request.
  • To administer, improve and secure the website.
  • To understand website performance and improve our marketing and content.
  • To keep appropriate business records and comply with legal obligations.

Legal bases

Depending on the circumstances, we process information because it is necessary to take steps at your request, because we have a legitimate business interest in responding to enquiries and operating the website, because you have given consent, or because we need to comply with a legal obligation.

Sharing information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website and with vending partners or service providers where this is reasonably necessary to assess or fulfil your enquiry. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including enquiry handling, business records, dispute resolution and legal or accounting requirements.

Cookies and analytics

The website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential site functions and analytics. Analytics information is used to understand how visitors use the site and to improve performance and content.

Your rights

Depending on applicable UK data protection law, you may have rights to ask for access to your personal information, correction, deletion, restriction, objection to certain processing, or transfer of information. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@vendingni.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with the updated date shown above.
