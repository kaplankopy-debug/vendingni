Refreshments that work around shifts

Industrial sites frequently operate before, after or beyond normal retail opening hours. A vending machine can give staff predictable access to food and drinks during breaks without requiring a staffed canteen for every shift.

Higher-footfall locations may suit larger-capacity equipment or separate snack and drink machines. Replenishment frequency can then be adjusted around actual demand.

Factory and warehouse demand

Multi-shift and 24/7 operations can create demand outside canteen or shop hours. Assessment considers workforce by shift, break timing, production access and the capacity needed between refill visits.

Recommended setup and placement

Higher-use sites may suit separate snack and drinks machines; a combination unit can suit a smaller break area. Robust, visible placement near shared breaks is normally more useful than an isolated position.

Managed service

Replenishment frequency follows usage, and compatible monitoring can assist planning. Send workforce per shift, hours, access, break locations and product needs.

Tell us about your location

Email the workforce by shift, operating hours, site location and required products. We can assess suitability using multi-shift network operating experience.
